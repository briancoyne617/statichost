EXERCISES DONE TODAY
MEDIAL / PINKY-SIDE ELBOW Prioritise the wrist flexion + pronation work — the flexor-pronator mass is the dynamic backup for the ligament you strained. On the mats, the straight posted arm is your highest-risk position, more than the submissions themselves. Everything here is gentle, frequent, and under your own muscle power. Nobody cranks the elbow toward straight.

Knee-to-wall

Toe to wall, heel down, knee touches wall. Slide the foot back 1cm at a time to the max you can hold.

PISTOL TARGET: 12–15 CM · ASYMMETRY < 1.5 CM

Single-leg calf raises

Barefoot, fingertips on a wall for balance only. Full height, controlled down, stop when height or form drops.

TARGET: 25 CLEAN REPS PER SIDE

Eyes-closed balance

Barefoot, one leg, eyes closed. Time until the free foot touches down. Best of three.

TARGET: 30 SECONDS PER SIDE

Elbow extension deficit

Both arms flat on a table, palms up, fully relaxed. Photograph from the side. Record degrees short of straight — your good arm is the target, not zero.

RECHECK WEEKLY · THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS MOST

Grip symmetry

Dynamometer, gripper reps, or a timed dead hang — same method every time.

TARGET: INJURED WITHIN 10% OF GOOD SIDE
